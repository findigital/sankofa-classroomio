title: Grade an Exercise

---

When students submit exercises, their work appears in the **Submissions** tab of the course dashboard. The board has three columns: **Submitted**, **Awaiting review**, and **Graded**. Auto-graded exercises usually skip to **Graded**. Exercises that need manual review usually appear in **Awaiting review** when the student submits.

## Grading workflow

| Stage               | Meaning                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Submitted**       | Handed in and not yet in review.                                                                               |
| **Awaiting review** | Waiting for instructor review. Open-ended or video exercises usually land here as soon as the student submits. |
| **Graded**          | Review is complete and the grade is recorded                                                                   |

Submissions are displayed as cards on a board. Drag a card between columns or open it to change its status manually.

:::info[Auto-graded exercises]
Exercises that use only objective question types (multiple choice, etc.) are auto-graded when submitted. They move directly to **Graded** without manual review. Exercises with open-ended or video questions require manual review and show **Needs manual review** on the card.
:::

## Grade a submission

### 1. Open the Submissions tab

From the course dashboard, click **Submissions** in the top navigation.

### 2. Open a submission

Click a submission card in **Submitted** or **Awaiting review**, depending on where it appears. The grading dialog opens showing the student's answers, a score breakdown, the hand-in time in your local timezone, and a comment field.

### 3. Review and score

Each question shows the student's answer alongside the expected answer or rubric. Scores are calculated automatically where possible. Adjust individual question scores if needed.

### 4. Leave a comment (optional)

Type feedback in **Add comment**. The student can see this after grading is complete.

### 5. Submit the grade

Click **Submit Grades**. The card moves to **Graded** and the student's score is recorded.

## AI grading availability

The current grading screen does not offer an active **Grade with AI** action. Review open-ended answers, enter scores and feedback manually, and select **Submit Grades**. See [Understand AI grading availability](/deliver-and-engage/grade-an-exercise-with-ai) for the current workflow.
