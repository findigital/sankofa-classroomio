Exercise submissions appear on the course's **Submissions** tab. A manually reviewed submission moves from **Submitted** to **In Progress** to **Reviewed**; objective questions can be scored automatically on submission.

## What each status means

- **Submitted**: the student has handed in work that has not been reviewed yet.
- **In Progress**: a tutor or administrator has opened it for review.
- **Reviewed**: the review is complete and a score has been recorded.

Open a card on the **Submissions** board to see answers, score details, and the feedback field. Review each answer, adjust scores where needed, and select **Submit review** to finalize the result. Open-ended or video answers may require manual review; exercises that score themselves can move directly to **Reviewed**.

Students can see feedback after grading completes. A course with a **Passed** exercise completion rule may keep later content locked until the grade meets the pass threshold. If a student is waiting, check both their submission status and the exercise's completion rule.
