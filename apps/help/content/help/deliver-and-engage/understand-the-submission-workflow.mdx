Exercise submissions appear on the course's **Submissions** tab. Manually reviewed submissions usually appear in **Awaiting review** when the student submits, then move to **Reviewed** after you submit grades. You can also drag cards between columns when you need to change status. Objective questions can be graded automatically on submission and then appear in **Reviewed**.

## What each status means

- **Submitted**: handed in and not yet in review.
- **Awaiting review**: the submission is waiting for instructor review. Exercises that need manual grading usually land here as soon as the student submits.
- **Reviewed**: the review is complete and a grade has been recorded.

Open a card on the **Submissions** board to see answers, score details, and the feedback field. Review each answer, adjust scores where needed, and select **Submit review** to finalize the result. Open-ended or video answers may require manual review; exercises that score themselves can move directly to **Reviewed**.

Students can see feedback after grading completes. A course with a **Passed** exercise completion rule may keep later content locked until the grade meets the pass threshold. If a student is waiting, check both their submission status and the exercise's completion rule.
