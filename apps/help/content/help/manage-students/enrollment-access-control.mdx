Organization signup policies control who can create an account, while course enrollment controls decide how students join a course. Review both before opening or restricting access.

## Set organization policies

Organization administrators open **Settings → Authentication → General**. Review these signup and catalog controls before saving:

- **Allow public signups:** On allows anyone to create an account. Off requires a valid invitation link to sign up.
- **Internal Enrollment Only:** On limits normal course self-enrollment to existing organization members. Invite outsiders when you want them to join.
- **Block all new signups:** Prevents new account creation, including invited students. Existing accounts are unaffected.
- **Members-only catalog:** When on, only members of this organization see courses on `/courses`. Everyone else sees an empty catalog. The academy homepage does not list courses. Visitors who are not signed in can sign in. Signed-in users who are not members still cannot see the courses.
- **Enroll members in every published course:** On by default while the members-only catalog is on. Students are added to every published course, including courses published later and students who join later. Unpublished courses stay hidden, and students are not added to them. Saving the setting, or the member enrollment backfill, also removes student memberships on unpublished courses when those students have no learning activity in that course, such as lesson progress, exercise submissions, attendance, or comments. Memberships with any recorded activity stay. Activity rows themselves are not deleted. Turn this off when students should not be added to every published course automatically. Welcome email per course is not sent; see [Welcome email](/manage-students/welcome-email).
- **Go to course:** Signed-in organization members see **Go to course** or **Continue** on the academy catalog and the public course page, with the price hidden, when the members-only catalog is on or member auto-enroll is on. The **Pricing** section and its tab are hidden for those members. When that page is visible to someone who is not an organization member, they see **Enroll Now** or **Buy Now**. The members-only catalog hides the catalog and the course page from everyone else. To show **Enroll Now** or **Buy Now** to members again, turn off both controls.
- **Hide course prices:** On by default. Hides prices, the pricing filter, and Free labels on the catalog and course cards until you turn it off. The public course page also hides its **Pricing** section and tab.
- **Show course type filter:** On by default. Shows course types that have at least one published course.
- **Catalog filter groups:** Choose which tag groups appear as catalog filters. Every group is included until you pick a subset.

The first two controls require an Early Adopter or Enterprise plan, or a self-hosted installation. Blocking all new signups is an Enterprise control in the hosted app. The catalog controls are available on every plan. A lock beside a control indicates that your current plan cannot change it.

:::warning[Check invitations before blocking signups]
A new student cannot create an account while **Block all new signups** is on, even when they have an invitation. Use this setting only when that is the intended result.
:::

Select **Save changes** after reviewing your choices. These policies apply to the current organization.

## Set course access

Open course **Settings → Access**:

- Turn **Students can enroll themselves** on for normal free-course enrollment, or off when students should receive access from your team.
- Turn **Publish Course** on when students should be able to see and open the course. Review lesson and exercise locks separately.

A paid course cannot use the normal free self-enrollment path. [Verify payment and grant access](/manage-learners/enroll-learners-in-a-paid-course).

## Choose an access policy

For open enrollment, allow public signups, turn off internal-only enrollment, and enable self-enrollment on the published free course.

For an internal academy, enable **Internal Enrollment Only**. Existing organization members can join courses that allow self-enrollment; outsiders need an invitation.

For invitation-based access, turn off course self-enrollment and distribute invitations only to intended students. Review [reusable invite links](/manage-learners/invite-learners-with-a-reusable-link), because anyone holding an active link can join.

## Test with a student account

Use a separate demo student to check signup, enrollment, and published content. An administrator's access does not prove what a student can open. To revoke an existing student's access, [remove their course membership](/manage-learners/add-and-remove-people-from-a-course) or [manage their organization membership](/manage-students/find-and-remove-inactive-students).
