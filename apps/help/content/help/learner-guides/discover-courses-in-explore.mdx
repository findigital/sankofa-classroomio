Select **Explore** in the student LMS to browse published courses available for discovery in your academy. Search filters the 12 courses loaded on the current page by title; check other pages if a course does not appear. Use the sort or view controls to scan the catalog.

![Explore catalog with published courses available to browse](./images/discover-courses-in-explore/overview.webp)

Select a card to preview the course, including its description, lesson and exercise counts, requirements, and displayed price. A free course with self-enrollment open shows **Enroll Now**; a paid one shows **Buy Now**. The button is disabled when the course is not accepting self-enrollment. After your academy enrolls you in a published course, that course leaves **Explore** and appears under **Home** and **My Learning**. **Go to course** and **Continue**, with the price hidden, appear on the academy catalog and the public course page, not in this preview.

Explore is a catalog, not a guarantee that you can join every listed course. Ask the course team for an invitation if enrollment is restricted. Once you join, the course moves to **Home** and **My Learning**.
