---
title: 'Enroll yourself in a course'
description: 'Join a course that allows self-enrollment.'
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

You can join a course yourself when its academy permits self-enrollment. If you are already a student and the academy enrolls members in every published course, you already have access. On the academy catalog and the public course page, choose **Go to course** or **Continue** and open the lessons. Skip **Enroll Now** and the join confirmation. The **Explore** preview still shows **Enroll Now** or **Buy Now** for courses you have not joined yet.

![Free course preview with the Enroll Now action](./images/enroll-yourself-in-a-course/overview.webp)

1. Open the academy catalog, a shared link, or your dashboard. **Explore** lists courses you have not joined.
2. Select the course and review its description, requirements, and price.
3. If the button says **Go to course** or **Continue**, open the lessons. Otherwise choose **Enroll Now** for a free course, or **Buy Now** for a paid course. Sign in or create an account if prompted.
4. On the enrollment page, select **Join course** and wait for confirmation. Skip this step when you already chose **Go to course** or **Continue**.
5. Find the course on **Home** or **My Learning**.

If the course says it is not accepting enrollment, contact its team for an invitation or direct assignment. A course can appear in **Explore** even when you cannot self-enroll. If a price is shown, use [Join a paid course](/learner-guides/join-a-paid-course) and review payment before confirming.

If the course does not appear after joining, refresh **My Learning** and confirm you signed in to the correct academy account.
